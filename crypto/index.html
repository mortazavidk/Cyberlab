Hashing and integrity check

Calculate SHA-256 for an original message, then compare it with a modified message. Even a small change should produce a different digest.

Awaiting comparison.
A matching hash can detect accidental changes, but a hash alone does not authenticate the sender. For adversarial tampering, use a MAC or digital signature.
